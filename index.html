The studio

Two developers.
One stubborn standard.

We're a two-person studio working out of California. There's no hand-off between design and engineering here, and no department to blame. The people who sketch a feature are the people who test it at 2 a.m.

So we pick problems other people call impossible (an audio recorder that survives a crash mid-take, a plug-in that prints to a hundredth of a point) and we keep going until they aren't.

How we build

Four rules we don't bend.

  1. 01

    Accuracy is the product.

    If a tool says 100%, it measures 100%. Artboard Print Sheet checks every placed sample after placement, never shrinks one to make it fit, and prints its own calibration ruler on every page.

  2. 02

    Private by default.

    No ads, no analytics and no sign-in. Audio Recorder contains no tracking libraries at all. Your recordings stay on your device unless you start the local Wi-Fi server yourself.

  3. 03

    Built for real life.

    Software meets incoming calls, full disks and dying batteries. Recordings are written to protected storage first and recovered after a crash, and a low battery stops a take cleanly instead of losing it.

  4. 04

    Fluent everywhere.

    Localisation isn't a translated menu. We handle plural rules, decimal separators, native numerals and per-script fonts, so the product feels built for each language rather than translated into it.
